Find your name, copy your signature, and paste it into Outlook. Each signature includes the GHP logo and company disclaimer.

How to set up your signature
  1. Find your signature below and select Copy signature.
  2. Open the signature settings in Outlook. Select your GHP account and paste into its signature editor, replacing the old signature.
  3. Save, then send a new email to check the logo, contact details, and spacing.

Using Outlook on your phone?

Open Outlook Settings → Mail → Signature. After pasting and saving, close and reopen the signature to check the logo. If it disappears, save the logo below to your photos, then insert it with the picture button in the signature editor. Send a test email to confirm it stays in place.
